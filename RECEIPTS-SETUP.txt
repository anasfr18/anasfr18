RECEIPTS

receipt.html = customer receipt
merchant-receipt.html = merchant/admin receipt

Checkout should save the final order object as `ebyan_last_order` in localStorage before
redirecting to receipt.html.

IMPORTANT:
This is a client-side receipt template. For production, the authoritative order and receipt
must be generated from the shared Supabase order record. Email delivery requires the configured
email backend (e.g. Resend/SMTP). M-Pesa receipts must remain pending until manually verified.
